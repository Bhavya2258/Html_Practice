My Dream Destination

Monaco

Monaco is a small, glamorous country located on the French Riviera. It is known for its luxurious lifestyle, beautiful Mediterranean coastline, and as a playground for the rich and famous. The country is famous for its casinos, yacht-lined harbor, and prestigious events such as the Monaco Grand Prix.

  1. Visit the Monte Carlo Casino
    • Try your luck at the gaming tables
    • Enjoy a fine dining experience at the casino's restaurant
  2. Explore the Prince's Palace of Monaco
    • Take a guided tour of the palace
    • Witness the changing of the guard ceremony
  3. Relax on Larvotto Beach
    • Sunbathe and swim in the Mediterranean Sea
    • Enjoy water sports and beachside cafes
  4. Attend the Monaco Grand Prix
    • Experience the thrill of Formula 1 racing
    • Watch the race from the grandstands or a luxury yacht
  5. Stroll through the Exotic Garden of Monaco
    • Admire the diverse collection of exotic plants
    • Enjoy panoramic views of the city and coastline
